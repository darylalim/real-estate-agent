RESIDENTIAL PURCHASE AGREEMENT

1. PARTIES. This Agreement is made between Kai Nakamura ("Buyer") and
Malia Okada ("Seller").

2. PROPERTY. The real property at 102 Kamehameha Ave, Hilo, HI 96720
(MLS-1085), a single-family residence of approximately 1,499 square feet,
together with all fixtures attached to it.

3. PURCHASE PRICE. The purchase price is $480,000, payable as follows:
an earnest money deposit of $15,000, a down payment of $81,000, and the
balance of $384,000 from a conventional mortgage loan that Buyer intends
to obtain.

4. EARNEST MONEY. Buyer shall deliver the earnest money deposit to escrow
within three (3) business days of acceptance. Upon acceptance, the earnest
money deposit is non-refundable to Buyer for any reason, including the
failure of any condition in this Agreement.

5. CLOSING. Closing shall occur on or before November 14, 2026, at an
escrow company selected by Seller.

6. INSPECTION. Buyer may, at Buyer's expense, have the Property inspected.
Buyer must deliver any written objection to the condition of the Property
within two (2) days after acceptance. If Buyer does not deliver a written
objection within that period, Buyer is deemed to have accepted the
Property in its present condition.

7. APPRAISAL. If the Property appraises below the purchase price, Buyer
may cancel this Agreement by written notice within five (5) days of
receiving the appraisal.

8. TITLE. Seller shall convey marketable title by warranty deed. Buyer
shall receive a preliminary title report within ten (10) days of
acceptance and may object to any defect in writing within five (5) days
of receiving it.

9. POSSESSION. Seller shall deliver possession of the Property to Buyer
forty-five (45) days after closing. Seller may occupy the Property during
that period.

10. CLOSING COSTS. Each party shall pay its own escrow fees. Seller shall
pay the Hawaii conveyance tax. Buyer shall pay for the lender's title
insurance policy and all loan fees.

11. DEFAULT. If Buyer defaults, Seller may retain the earnest money
deposit as liquidated damages. If Seller defaults, Buyer may seek any
remedy available at law or in equity.

12. DISPUTES. Any dispute arising under this Agreement shall first be
submitted to mediation in Hawaii County before any action is filed.

Buyer: Kai Nakamura          Seller: Malia Okada
